My 2017 Subaru BRZ is a simple, rear-wheel-drive sports car. I like it for how it feels to drive, rather than how it looks on a spec sheet. Conveniently, so does the spec sheet.

ModificationsNone — factory configuration (a rare condition on the internet)

Why I like it

The seating position, steering feel and chassis balance are what make it interesting. It is enjoyable at ordinary road speeds without needing huge amounts of power. The car takes this philosophy very seriously.

I'd rather understand the car in its original form before spending money on modifications. The shopping cart can wait. Probably.

Possible future changes

  • Tyres suited to how I use the car.
  • Brake pads and fluid when appropriate.
  • An alignment that fits the car's intended use.
  • Wheels or suspension only if there is a good reason.

These are ideas, not completed modifications. Maintenance comes first, even when shiny car parts make a compelling financial argument (they do not).
