Training

I'm interested in evidence-informed lifting, especially hypertrophy. My aim is to train consistently, make measurable progress, and manage fatigue. Essentially, spreadsheets with a pump.

Training principles

Effort
Make working sets challenging. Not every set needs to end in muscular failure. The weights do not need a dramatic finale.
Exercise selection
Choose movements that are comfortable, repeatable and suited to the muscles I'm trying to train.
Progression
Record loads, reps and technique. Look for improvements over time rather than forcing a personal record every session. Unfortunately, muscles do not accept quarterly performance targets.
Volume and recovery
Do enough work to progress, and adjust if fatigue is consistently getting in the way. Sleep, food and time between sessions matter. Recovery is part of the programme, not an optional DLC.

What I try to track

  • Exercise, working weight, sets and repetitions.
  • Estimated reps in reserve (RIR).
  • Technique and range of motion.
  • Recovery and performance from one session to the next.

People I learn from

  • Jeff Nippard — strength and hypertrophy education.
  • Renaissance Periodization / Dr. Mike Israetel — programming and fatigue management.

These are personal training notes, not an individualized training prescription. I am not legally or spiritually responsible for your leg day.
