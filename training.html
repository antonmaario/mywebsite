// methodology

Gym decisions,
but with fewer vibes.

Not every training question has one perfect answer, so I prefer useful heuristics over pretending the literature has a magic split hidden somewhere.

stimulus

Train close enough

Hard sets matter. So does knowing when another set is just adding fatigue with very little return.

selection

Pick movements that fit

Exercises are tools, not personality tests. Stable, repeatable, targetable movements are usually a good deal.

progress

Write it down

If reps, load, execution, and recovery are never tracked, “progressive overload” becomes a motivational quote.

recovery

Adaptation happens outside

Training is the stimulus. Food, sleep, stress management, and not doing twelve extra sets because the playlist was good finish the job.
