// ctf loop

The scientific method,
but louder.

I am not pretending to be a movie hacker. CTFs are mostly curiosity, enumeration, documentation, rabbit holes, and eventually realizing the clue was right there.

01. Enumerate everything

Ports, services, versions, files, weird behavior. Collect facts before writing fan fiction about the exploit chain.

02. Form a theory

Pick the suspicious thing and test it. Accept that the suspicious thing may be completely innocent.

03. Read documentation

The forbidden technique. Extremely effective.

04. Get flag / get humbled

Both count as learning. Only one produces dopamine immediately.

// rule zero

Keep the security stuff legal.

Labs, CTF platforms, systems I own, or systems I have explicit permission to test. Breaking into random things is not a personality trait.
