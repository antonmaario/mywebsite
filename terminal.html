Linux & tech

I use Fedora on my laptop and enjoy learning how systems work. My interests include Linux, networking, shell scripting and cybersecurity. Sometimes the troubleshooting is the hobby.

Linux

Fedora is my distribution of choice. I like learning through everyday use, troubleshooting and reading documentation. Occasionally in that order, which explains a lot.

Two simple commands I find useful:

cat /etc/os-release
sudo dnf upgrade

The first shows distribution information; the second checks for and applies package updates after confirmation. Very glamorous stuff.

Security labs

I occasionally use capture-the-flag (CTF) challenges to practise problem-solving. I try to understand the problem, keep notes, consult documentation and learn from mistakes. The last step offers an exceptionally generous learning opportunity.

I only practise on authorized labs, systems I own, or systems I have explicit permission to test.
